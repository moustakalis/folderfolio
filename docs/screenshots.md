# The wp.org screenshots

Eight PNGs in `.wordpress-org/` (`screenshot-1.png` … `screenshot-8.png`), taken on 25 Sep 2026 for 1.0.0.

## How they were made

- **Where:** a clean WordPress Playground at playground.wordpress.net (WordPress 7.1.2, PHP 8.3, English), in Comet through Claude in Chrome. Never the dev site.
- **Plugin:** the ZIP built from `main` by `bin/stage-plugin.sh`, uploaded through *Plugins → Add Plugin → Upload*.
- **Site:** *Saltwater & Stone*, a travel and food journal. 16 media folders (three levels under *Campaigns*), 4 post folders, 7 posts (two drafts), one page with the Folder gallery block. Colours on six folders, *Campaigns* pinned, *Archive* locked, *Still life* a gallery, *Coast* and *Interiors* starred, two smart folders.
- **Pixels:** each shot is 2× — the admin page in a same-origin iframe at the stated CSS size (1440 × 900 for the library, 1440 × 660 for Posts, 1200 × 900 for Import, 1140 × 1215 for Settings, 390 × 844 for the phone), moved under the viewport in tiles captured at the display's native resolution and stitched; overlaps checked pixel-identical. The editor (7) was taken top-level at 1456 × 628, because the block editor will not load inside that iframe. Shot 8 is two phone captures side by side on a plain ground.
